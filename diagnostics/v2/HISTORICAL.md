# Historical diagnostic — do not use for new measurements

Use [v4](../v4/GUIDE-v4.md). These files preserve the development record. In particular v3 read SEC2 slot 9 at 0x824160 and incorrectly called it GSP FWSEC. Its FWSEC/version-1 conclusion is withdrawn. Correct FWSEC slot 9 is 0x8241e0; raw 3 selects version 2. See [pitfalls](../../docs/PITFALLS.md).
